# StopLookAround.com

A travel photo community for people who love places more than selfies.

No faces. No accounts. No follower counts. Just places.

## Sections
- Photo wall — community submitted travel photos
- The Graffiti Wall — thoughts and memories from travelers
- It's Your Duty Free — free AI travel country sheets
- I Need a Bigger Bag — curated affiliate travel gear

## Deploy
Single HTML file. GitHub Pages hosted.
