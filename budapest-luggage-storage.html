Where to leave your bags in Budapest

Arriving before check-in or leaving hours after checkout? Ask your hotel first. If you’re taking a train later, a locker at your departure station can save a trip across town.

Keleti station

Look for lockers opposite the Customer Centre on the underpass level. MÁV lists access from 03:45 to 01:00 on weekdays and 03:45 to 02:00 on weekends. The larger lockers measure 50 × 60 × 75 cm inside. Check the current price at the station before storing your bag.

Nyugati station

MÁV lists lockers at the end of platform 6, beside the ticket machines, with access from 02:30 to 00:50 daily. The larger lockers measure 50 × 60 × 75 cm inside. Measure your case with the wheels and handles included and check the price at the station.

Nyugati station details from MÁV

Déli station

If your train leaves from Déli on the Buda side, look for lockers in the passenger hall. Enter near the international ticket office and turn left. MÁV lists access daily from 03:00 until 00:30 the following morning. The larger lockers measure 50 × 60 × 75 cm inside. Check the price at the station.

Budapest Airport

Excess Baggage Company lists its Budapest location on the Terminal 2 arrivals level. The airport directs travelers to the baggage-wrapping desk on the Terminal 2B departures level outside the main desk’s hours. Check where to collect your bags at your planned pickup time and ask for the current rate before leaving them. See the operator’s airport locations.

Central Pest near the Opera

Lion’s Locker lists storage at Vasvári Pál utca 11 for €5 per bag per day. Its posted hours are 08:00–19:00 Saturday through Monday and 08:00–18:00 Tuesday through Friday. It’s about a 10-minute walk from Deák Ferenc tér, according to the operator. Check holiday hours and leave time to collect your bags.

Your hotel or accommodation

Ask whether the property can hold your bags before check-in or after checkout, whether there’s a fee, and how late you can collect them. If you booked a self-check-in apartment, ask your host before counting on storage.

Keep your passport, medication, and anything you’ll need before pickup with you. Build in time to collect your bags before your train or airport transfer.
