France Country Sheet

BEST PLACES
TO VISIT IN FRANCE

Paris is the beginning, not the whole country.

THE RULE: Do not build the trip around Paris alone. France rewards regional travel.

THE SHORT VERSION

This is a country to experience by regions, not a checklist of famous stops. Choose a strong base, leave room for slower places, and let the route matter as much as the headline sights.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Paris

Give it time: neighborhoods, markets, museums, and long walks beat a landmark sprint.

Provence

Hill towns, Roman remains, lavender country, and outdoor markets.

Normandy

D-Day history, tidal Mont-Saint-Michel, dairy country, and a hard-edged coast.

Alsace

Half-timbered towns, vineyard roads, and a culture shaped by France and Germany.

The French Alps

Annecy, Chamonix, and mountain country that works well beyond ski season.

Best time: April to June and September to October balance decent weather with lighter crowds.

Best first base: Paris for a first visit; Lyon for food and rail connections; Nice for the Riviera.

Pace: Give a major city at least three nights and avoid changing hotels every day.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Start with two anchors and connect them by rail when practical. Add one smaller city, island, valley, or rural stop instead of stacking more capitals. That creates a trip with contrast and leaves enough time to notice where you are.
