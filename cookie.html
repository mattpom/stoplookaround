Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

StopLookAround.com uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our site.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you use it.

How We Use Cookies

  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our site. This data is anonymized and aggregated.
  • Functionality: Some cookies are essential for the site to function correctly, such as remembering quiz scores and photo wall submissions.
  • Affiliate tracking: Affiliate links (Amazon, Awin network) may place cookies to track referrals and commissions.

Third-Party Cookies

Our affiliate partners including Amazon Associates, Booking.com, GetYourGuide, and Viator may set their own cookies when you click affiliate links on our site. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the site. You can also opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
