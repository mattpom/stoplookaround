Jordan Destination Guide

JORDAN

Ancient cities, desert silence, generous tables, and one of the world's great archaeological sites.

Before you book: Verify current regional advisories, entry rules, border status, desert conditions, and site hours before departure.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Amman

Use the capital for the Citadel, Roman Theatre, neighborhood food, and a grounded introduction to contemporary Jordan.

Jerash

Walk one of the region's most extensive Roman-era city sites; arrive early and allow more than a rushed hour.

Petra

Stay nearby and give the site a full day—preferably two—because the Treasury is only the beginning.

Wadi Rum

Choose a responsible local camp or guide and leave time for the desert beyond a quick photo stop.

The Dead Sea

Treat it as a short landscape and swimming stop, checking current access and water conditions before going.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

A practical first trip links Amman and Jerash with Petra, Wadi Rum, and the Dead Sea. Allow at least a week so the route does not become a sequence of long drives.

TURN THIS GUIDE INTO A TRIP

Check current official requirements first. Then compare a practical base and one experience worth building a day around.
