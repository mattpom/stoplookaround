Czech Republic Country Sheet

BEST PLACES
TO VISIT IN CZECH REPUBLIC

Prague earns the attention. The country beyond it earns the extra days.

THE RULE: Use the country name Czechia in ordinary speech if you like; both Czechia and Czech Republic are official.

THE SHORT VERSION

This is a country to experience by regions, not a checklist of famous stops. Choose a strong base, leave room for slower places, and let the route matter as much as the headline sights.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Prague

Architecture, river views, historic neighborhoods, and early mornings before the crowds.

Český Krumlov

A compact Renaissance town in a deep bend of the Vltava.

Kutná Hora

Gothic architecture, mining history, and the famous Sedlec Ossuary.

Moravia

Wine villages, karst landscapes, and the energetic city of Brno.

Karlovy Vary

A spa town of colonnades, forest walks, and grand hotels.

Best time: April to June and September to October; winter is atmospheric but daylight is short.

Best first base: Prague with one or two overnight regional stops.

Pace: Give a major city at least three nights and avoid changing hotels every day.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Start with two anchors and connect them by rail when practical. Add one smaller city, island, valley, or rural stop instead of stacking more capitals. That creates a trip with contrast and leaves enough time to notice where you are.
