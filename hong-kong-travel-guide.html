Hong Kong Destination Guide

HONG KONG

Dense city energy, mountain trails, ferries, markets, and its own travel logic.

Before you book: Hong Kong maintains its own immigration controls and currency. Entry requirements and local rules can change, so verify official information before travel.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Hong Kong Island

Central, Sheung Wan, the Peak, trams, markets, and dense vertical neighborhoods.

Kowloon

Harbor views, Temple Street, museums, and some of the city's most intense street life.

Lantau Island

Villages, trails, beaches, the Big Buddha, and the airport connection.

Outlying islands

Lamma and Cheung Chau offer ferries, waterfront walks, and a slower pace.

The New Territories

Walled villages, wetlands, hiking routes, and a less familiar side of Hong Kong.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Base near an MTR station and use ferries whenever they make sense. Three to five days gives the city and one quieter excursion room to breathe.

TURN THIS GUIDE INTO A TRIP

Check current official requirements first. Then compare a practical base and one experience worth building a day around.
