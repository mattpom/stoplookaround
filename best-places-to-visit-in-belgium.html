Belgium Country Sheet

BEST PLACES
TO VISIT IN BELGIUM

A small country with dense cities, serious food, and no need to rush.

THE RULE: Stay overnight in Bruges or Ghent. The atmosphere changes when day visitors leave.

THE SHORT VERSION

This is a country to experience by regions, not a checklist of famous stops. Choose a strong base, leave room for slower places, and let the route matter as much as the headline sights.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Brussels

Art Nouveau, major museums, comic-book culture, and neighborhoods beyond Grand-Place.

Bruges

Canals and medieval streets that are best early, late, or overnight.

Ghent

A lived-in university city with monumental architecture and real energy.

Antwerp

Fashion, Rubens, a major port, and one of Europe's great railway stations.

The Ardennes

Forests, river valleys, small towns, and important wartime history.

Best time: April to June and September; December is strong for festive city breaks.

Best first base: Brussels or Ghent puts most major cities within easy rail reach.

Pace: Give a major city at least three nights and avoid changing hotels every day.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Start with two anchors and connect them by rail when practical. Add one smaller city, island, valley, or rural stop instead of stacking more capitals. That creates a trip with contrast and leaves enough time to notice where you are.
