# Stop. Look Around. — Style Guide

Domain: stoplookaround.com. Repo: mattpom/stoplookaround.

## Identity

A no-faces travel-photo community, described as the anti-Instagram. Two rules define the whole brand: no faces, name the place.

## Voice and Tone

Playful, a little rebellious, short and punchy sentences. Uses repetition and rhythm as a stylistic device, reinforcing the no-selfies rule again and again.

## Color Palette

Cream f7f2e8, warm white fdfaf4, ink text 1c1c1c. Rust accent c4522a, mustard d4a843, sage 7a9e7e, dusty 8c7b6b, light-dust e8e0d4.

## Typography

Display and headline type uses Bebas Neue. Editorial and serif accents use Lora. Body and UI text use Karla and Barlow.

## Navigation

Explore, Duty Free, The Wall, Guides, Feed Me, World Music, Sleep Here, Gift Shop, Culture, Quiz, Deeper Dive, Upload.

## Layout Patterns

Anonymous, faceless photo submissions are presented as a scrolling community wall called The Wall. A repeating marquee-style ticker of rules and taglines runs as a design motif. Captions lead with location (place name plus country) instead of a user handle.

## Deeper Dive

Internal page at /deeper-dive.html, embeds the shared Pinterest profile widget. Present in nav on every page with a real nav; listed in sitemap.xml.
