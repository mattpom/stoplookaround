Russia Destination Guide

MOSCOW & ST. PETERSBURG

Two capitals, two versions of Russia, and a trip that requires current information.

Before you book: Travel advisories, visa rules, payment access, transport options, insurance coverage, and consular support can change quickly. Check your government's current official guidance before considering or booking travel.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Moscow

The Kremlin, Red Square, major museums, historic neighborhoods, and an extraordinary metro system.

St. Petersburg

The Hermitage, imperial avenues, canals, palaces, and Russia's strongest concentration of European-facing history.

Golden Ring option

If conditions and time permit, add a historic town such as Suzdal or Vladimir rather than another rushed city stop.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Use Moscow and St. Petersburg as the two anchors. The classic connection is by rail, but schedules and booking access must be verified at the time of travel.

TURN THIS GUIDE INTO A TRIP

Check current official requirements first. Then compare a practical base and one experience worth building a day around.
