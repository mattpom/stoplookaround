Russia Destination Guide

MOSCOW & ST. PETERSBURG

Two capitals, two versions of Russia, and a trip that requires current information.

Current U.S. travel advisory: The U.S. Department of State says, “Do not travel to Russia for any reason,” citing wrongful-detention risk, arbitrary enforcement of local laws, terrorism, the war in Ukraine, and limited consular assistance. U.S.-issued credit and debit cards do not work in Russia. Read the current official advisory → This guide provides cultural context and is not a recommendation to travel.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Moscow

The Kremlin, Red Square, major museums, historic neighborhoods, and an extraordinary metro system.

St. Petersburg

The Hermitage, imperial avenues, canals, palaces, and Russia's strongest concentration of European-facing history.

Golden Ring option

If conditions and time permit, add a historic town such as Suzdal or Vladimir rather than another rushed city stop.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Use Moscow and St. Petersburg as the two anchors. The classic connection is by rail, but schedules and booking access must be verified at the time of travel.

CHECK CURRENT CONDITIONS

Do not use booking or tour links as a substitute for official guidance. Review your government's current advisory, entry requirements, payment restrictions, insurance exclusions, and available consular support before making any decision.
