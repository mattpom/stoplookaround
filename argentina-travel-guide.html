Argentina Destination Guide

ARGENTINA

Big-city energy, wine country, waterfalls, and landscapes that run to the end of the map.

Before you book: Verify current entry rules, domestic-flight logistics, seasonal conditions, and payment guidance before departure.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Buenos Aires

Give the capital several days for neighborhood walks, cafés, markets, architecture, football culture, and late dinners.

Iguazú Falls

See the Argentine side for the trail network and close waterfall views; allow time for heat, spray, and changing park access.

Mendoza

Build the visit around wine country and the Andes, using reserved transportation rather than improvising between distant vineyards.

Patagonia

Choose a specific base—such as El Calafate, El Chaltén, or Bariloche—according to season and the landscapes you actually want.

Salta and the northwest

Colonial cities, high-altitude roads, colored mountains, and a regional culture distinct from Buenos Aires and Patagonia.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Use Buenos Aires as the cultural anchor, then choose one or two regions rather than trying to cover the country at once. Argentina's distances are substantial.

TURN THIS GUIDE INTO A TRIP

Check current official requirements first. Then compare a practical base and one experience worth building a day around.
