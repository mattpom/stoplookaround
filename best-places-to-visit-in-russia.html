Russia Country Sheet

BEST PLACES
TO VISIT IN RUSSIA

Extraordinary history and scale, but current conditions make advance research essential.

THE RULE: Do not plan from an old guidebook. Verify advisories, visas, payment access, insurance, transport, and border rules immediately before booking.
Before you book: Important: travel advisories, consular support, payment access, transportation, and border conditions may change rapidly. Consult your government's current official guidance before considering travel.

THE PLACES WORTH YOUR TIME

Moscow

The Kremlin, major museums, monumental public spaces, and one of the world's most distinctive metro systems.

St. Petersburg

Imperial architecture, the Hermitage, canals, and the country's strongest concentration of European-facing history.

The Golden Ring

Historic towns northeast of Moscow known for monasteries, churches, and traditional architecture.

Lake Baikal

The world's deepest lake and the center of one of Russia's most remarkable natural regions.

The Trans-Siberian route

A vast rail corridor rather than a single attraction, requiring serious time and logistical planning.

Best time: Late spring through early autumn offers the easiest conditions for most city travel; winter requires deliberate preparation.

Best first base: Moscow and St. Petersburg form the classic first route, when current travel conditions permit.

HOW TO BUILD THE TRIP

Choose two anchors and one contrasting regional stop. Leave room for longer transfers, language and payment setup, and the practical requirements that can make travel here different from a typical Western European trip.
