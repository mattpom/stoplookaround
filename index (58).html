WHAT WE DO
BEHIND THE CURTAIN

🧹

We Strip Your Metadata

Every photo uploaded has its EXIF data removed before it goes live — including GPS coordinates that could reveal exactly where you live or were standing.

👁️

Every Photo is Reviewed

Nothing goes live automatically. A real human checks every submission before it hits the wall. Faces, kids, anything sketchy — it doesn't get through.

🚸

No Kids. Ever.

Photos containing children — even from behind, even partially — are not accepted. Full stop. This isn't negotiable and never will be.

🔒

Your Photo Stays Yours

We do not own, claim, or want ownership of anything you upload. Your photo is yours the moment you take it and stays yours forever. We're just the wall it hangs on — nothing more.

On ownership: StopLookAround.com makes no claim of ownership over any photo submitted to this site. All images remain the sole intellectual property of the person who took them. By submitting a photo you grant us permission to display it here — that's it. You can request removal of your photo at any time and we will take it down, no questions asked.
