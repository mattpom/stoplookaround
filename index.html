We Review Every Photo

Nothing goes live automatically. A real human checks every submission before it hits the wall. If it doesn't belong here, it doesn't get through.

🚸

No Kids. Ever.

Photos containing children — even from behind, even partially — are not accepted. Full stop. This isn't negotiable and never will be.

🔒

Your Photo Stays Yours

We do not own, claim, or want ownership of anything you upload. Your photo is yours the moment you take it and stays yours forever. We're just the wall it hangs on.
